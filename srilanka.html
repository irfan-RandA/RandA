Sri Lanka

One island.
Many worlds.

From ancient cities and mountain tea country to wildlife parks and tropical beaches, Sri Lanka rewards travelers who leave room for discovery.

Inbound tours

Arrive ready to explore.

Sri Lanka welcomes international visitors through Colombo’s main international gateway, with flight options varying by season and origin. Travelers can combine a private island circuit with beach stays, cultural sites, wildlife and the central highlands.

Where to go

Routes with character.

Colombo

Urban energy, dining, shopping and a practical start or finish to the island.

Kandy

Temple heritage, hill-country gateway, lake views and cultural experiences.

Nuwara Eliya

Tea estates, cool weather, scenic valleys and highland roads.

Sigiriya

Ancient heritage, rock landscapes, village experiences and the Cultural Triangle.

Trincomalee

East-coast beaches, sea excursions and a relaxed tropical pace.

Yala

Wildlife drives, open landscapes and a chance to experience Sri Lanka’s natural side.

Mirissa

Southern-coast sunsets, beach time and access to coastal experiences.

Galle & Bentota

Colonial heritage, river journeys and easy access to west and south coast stays.
